Beast Array — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026
Developer: Martin Ho
Contact: beastarray@mail.grokbot.com

In short

  • The game works offline. There is no account and no sign-up.
  • Your progress and settings stay only on your device. We have no server of our own and receive no game data.
  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Google may collect your device's advertising ID and some device data to show and measure ads.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details.
  • The game has no analytics or crash-reporting SDK and no cloud save.
  • You must be 13 or older to use the game.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress, your settings and the result of the age check (a simple flag) on your device. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the game removes it.

Ads

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob. When ads load, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and use the advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and data about how ads are shown and clicked, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. We do not receive this data.

Personalised ads are shown only if you agree: first in the consent form (Google UMP) where it is shown, such as in the EEA and the UK, and then in the iOS "Allow tracking" (ATT) prompt. If you decline, or if you are aged 13 to 15, the game shows only non-personalised ads.

You can change your choice at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, where you can also reset or limit your advertising identifier. Google explains how it handles data in Google's privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The one-time Remove Ads purchase turns off the ads between matches. The optional rewarded ads (watched only if you choose, to get hint tickets) still work, so the ads SDK can still run.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store and handled by Apple under Apple's own terms and privacy policy. We do not receive your name, card or payment details. The game only knows whether Remove Ads has been bought.

What we do not do

We have no account system and no server of our own. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. We do not offer cloud save. We do not sell personal data.

Children

The game is for people aged 13 and over. When you first open it, it asks when you were born. This answer is not sent to us. If you are under 13, the game does not let you continue and you must not use it. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has sent us information, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have the right to ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or to object to how it is used. We hold almost nothing about you: the game keeps its data on your device, and the only personal data we may hold is the emails you send us. To use a right, email us. Data held by Google or Apple is handled by them; contact them or use the controls in iPhone Settings.

International transfers

We have no server. Ad data is processed by Google, which may process it in countries other than yours under Google's own safeguards. Purchases are processed by Apple.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the date above.
