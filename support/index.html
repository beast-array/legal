Quick answers

How do I remove ads?

Buy the one-time “Remove Ads” purchase in the game. It removes the ads shown between matches. The optional rewarded ads, which you watch only if you choose to get hint tickets, stay available. Purchases are handled by Apple.

How do I restore my purchase?

If you reinstalled the game or changed iPhone, use “Restore Purchases” in the game at Settings → Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID that bought it.

How do I delete my data?

The game keeps its data only on your device. Delete the app to remove it. See how to remove your data.

Can I move my progress to a new iPhone?

There is no account and no cloud save, so we cannot restore or move progress for you.

What age is the game for?

You must be 13 or older to use the game.
