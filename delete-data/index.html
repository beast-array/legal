Beast Array — How to Remove Your Data

Last updated: 26 October 2026
Developer: Martin Ho
Contact: beastarray@mail.grokbot.com

Beast Array has no account and no server of our own. Your progress, settings and age-check result are stored only on your device.

  1. Delete the game data. Delete the app from your iPhone: Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Beast Array → Delete App (not Offload App). This removes what the game saved on your device. It cannot be undone, and there is no cloud save to restore from.
  2. Advertising ID. You can reset or limit your advertising identifier and stop apps from asking to track you in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Ad data held by Google is managed through Google's own settings.
  3. Purchases. Your purchase record is kept by Apple, not by us. You can see it in your App Store purchase history; we cannot delete it.
  4. Questions about your data. Email beastarray@mail.grokbot.com. The only personal data we may hold is the emails you send us. We will delete them on request unless the law requires us to keep them.
