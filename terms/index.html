Beast Array — Terms of Use (summary)

Last updated: 26 October 2026
Developer: Martin Ho
Contact: beastarray@mail.grokbot.com

This page is a short summary in plain language. The binding licence for the app is Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement (EULA), which applies to apps from the App Store: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/. We do not publish custom terms.

The main points

  • Age. You must be 13 or older to use the game.
  • Free with ads. The game is free to download and play. It shows ads, including ads between matches and optional rewarded ads. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Remove Ads. An optional one-time purchase that removes the ads shown between matches. It does not remove the optional rewarded ads you can watch to get hint tickets. Purchases are handled by Apple, and refunds follow Apple's rules.
  • Offline. The game plays offline on your device. The internet is only needed for ads and purchases.
  • No account. There is nothing to register or sign in to. Progress is stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes it, and there is no cloud save, so we cannot restore it.
  • Licence terms. The rules on use, warranty, liability and governing law are those in Apple's EULA.

Changes

We may update this summary. The new version will be posted on this page.
